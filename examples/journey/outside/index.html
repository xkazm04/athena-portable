For our suppliers

Kestrel Labs settles supplier invoices on the first working Tuesday after the due date. Remittance advice is published here rather than emailed, so the reference against a payment can always be checked against the payment itself.

Two of our programmes hold parallel retainers with the same suppliers. Payments for both leave the same account under the name KESTREL LABS, so the remittance reference is the only thing that says which retainer a payment settled.
