# Design System: tidycrm

Produced by following the `google-labs-code/stitch-skills` DESIGN.md discipline. The repo ships two
DESIGN.md formats: `stitch-utilities/design-md` (5 sections — atmosphere, colour, type, components,
layout) and `stitch-utilities/taste-design` (the same, plus Hero, Responsive, Motion and a banned
list). This file uses the 9-section `taste-design` format, because the 5-section one has no spacing,
no motion and no scales of any kind and could not describe this app.

**This file is law, not values.** The values live in
`components/blocks/style/base/tokens.css`, scoped to `[data-variant="blocks"]` and imported first by
`components/blocks/style/index.css`. Where a number below is quoted it is quoted as a *rule about*
the token, and the token is the authority. Nothing here re-declares a hex that the token file owns.

> **Divergence from the skill.** `taste-design` assumes one design system per project, and that is
> now what this app has: one direction, `blocks`, and it is the app: it was promoted from `/v/blocks`
> to the root route when the direction index was deleted. Sections 1, 2, 3 and 5 are written
> for it; sections 4, 6, 7, 8 and 9 are shared law that any direction in this app obeys. The
> divergence is kept because the `/v` experiment is not over by argument — it is over by review, and
> the law has to outlive the directions it was written against. §11 records the ones that were tried
> and cut, and where their material went.

## 0. Dials

`taste-design`'s four dials — Creativity, Density, Variance, Motion — are the only mechanism in the
reference that *generates* a direction rather than describing one. Five of the directions this file
has described were generated that way. The one that ships was not.

| Direction | Route | Generated by | One line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`blocks`** | `/` | the written design law of `design/pass3-law-brief.md`, followed | One check print read at three depths: a quartered cube of records, a zone as a ruled row list, a block as a dossier. |

`blocks` follows the `law` direction, which declined the dial table outright and substituted
hirelane's `examples/hirelane/DESIGN-LAW.md` brief-inference fields (`design/pass3-law-brief.md` §0
records that ruling clause by clause, and `design/pass3-dials-brief.md` §1.1 confirms that neither
`law` nor its two pass-3 siblings adopted the dials). So there are no dial settings to quote, and
inventing a row for it would be the one thing this file forbids: a value with no authority behind it.

The dial table is kept as a *mechanism* — if a direction is ever wanted again, survey the occupied
corners in `design/pass3-dials-brief.md` §1 and take an unoccupied one. The five rows it used to hold
are in §11.

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

### `blocks` — "The Blocks"

The drawing-office check print: a graphite-on-vellum general arrangement drawing returned from the
checker. Hatched survey blocks, an inverted title block in the corner carrying the revision and every
irreversible act, **redline** marking what deviates from the specification and **greenline**
confirming what has been checked. Paper is `--bk-vellum` and `--bk-sheet`; there are no shadows
anywhere (`--bk-elev-0: none`) and no rounded corner on anything (`--bk-radius-0: 0px`), because
depth on a drawing sheet is rule weight, inversion and hatch density.

What this direction tests is not a second opinion about lettering — it deliberately shares `law`'s
palette, type and polarity — but **structure**: the same survey read at three depths, on one object.
At L0 the whole sheet is a single three.js cube quartered into the four lettered zones, one dot per
record inside it, graphite where nothing is outstanding, redline where the record deviates and gold
where it stands in an identity pair no rule may resolve. Opening a quadrant flattens those dots onto
a plate and the zone arrives on top of them as a ruled row list of its blocks. Opening a block raises
its dossier over the sheet: the verdict, the four checks, the offending rows, the identity pair, and
the merge behind the inverted title block.

The atmosphere is still. Nothing pulses, nothing spins, and nothing implies work is happening —
because Athena is not connected to this app and a surface that breathes reads as one that is
thinking.

## 2. Colour Palette & Roles

Authority: `components/blocks/style/base/tokens.css`. Every colour is emitted as a `--bk-*` custom
property scoped to `[data-variant="blocks"]`, and the block ends with an alias layer that re-points
the kit's `--dk-*` hooks at those values, so the shared toast, manifest drawer and activity log
follow the direction without knowing it exists. Alias, never fork.

**Rules that bind any direction in this app** (from `taste-design` §2, adopted):

- Exactly **one** accent per direction. No second "also accent".
- **Never `#000000`.** `blocks` bottoms out at `--bk-graphite`, a warm near-black.
- **The "AI purple/blue neon" aesthetic is banned** — no purple button glows, no neon gradient fills.
  A hue that means nothing is decoration and is banned. Every hue-bearing token in `blocks` is bound
  to a named finding: redline to a deviation from the specification, greenline to a check that
  passed, and one declared addition, `--bk-goldline`, to an identity pair nobody has adjudicated.
  That third hue is argued for in the token file's own header, which is where a deviation from a
  colour lock has to be written down.
- One neutral family per direction. The graphite ramp is four steps plus a rule ink; no
  warm-grey/cool-grey fluctuation inside it.

**Rules this app adds, which `taste-design` has no concept of:** governance is a colour role, not a
badge, and its roles may not be spent on anything else.

| Role | Token | Meaning — fixed for every direction |
|---|---|---|
| auto | `--dk-auto` | Reversible. The agent may do this unattended. |
| gated | `--dk-gated` | Irreversible. A human must arm it first. |
| destructive | `--bk-redline` | The fired state of a gated action. |

`merge_contacts` and `delete_contacts` are GATED because they destroy identity and cannot be replayed
backwards, and `undo` is GATED with them because `undoActivity` writes its own row `reversible: false`
— an undo is not itself undoable. `normalize_fields`, `flag_stale` and `resolve_pair` are AUTO
**because every write lands in `revisions` or `merge_pairs` and undo replays it**, not because they
are harmless. Reversibility buys the AUTO class. A direction that colours these roles by aesthetic
preference has broken the app.

`blocks` binds `--dk-auto` to greenline and answers the gated role with **a place rather than a
hue**: the two irreversible acts live in the inverted title block, bottom-right, and `--dk-gated` is
graphite. Only the fired control takes a colour, and only while the pointer is on it
(`components/blocks/style/level2/foot.css:92-95`). That is a declared reading of the rule, not an
exemption from it — the role still means one thing and is still spent on nothing else.

## 3. Typography Rules

`taste-design` bans `Inter` outright and bans serif "in dashboards or software UIs regardless".

**Adopted:** the `Inter` ban. **Rejected:** the blanket serif ban. The skill's own escape hatch is the
correct reading — it permits a chosen serif and bans only the default browser stacks (`Times`,
`Georgia`, `Garamond`). A check print has two voices and the second one is a serif: what the office
*printed* is a drafted sans, what a person *wrote* in the margin is a note face.

| Direction | Printed (display / UI) | Written (margin notes) | Figures |
|---|---|---|---|
| `blocks` | Archivo, width axis (`--bk-font-draft`) | Literata, optical-size axis (`--bk-font-note`) | Martian Mono, width axis, tabular (`--bk-font-fig`) |

All three faces are self-hosted through `next/font` at the module scope of the page that uses them
(`app/page.tsx`). No runtime font requests, per repo law.

**Scale rules** (values in `components/blocks/style/base/tokens.css` as `--bk-text-*`,
`--bk-leading-*`, `--bk-track-*`):

- Display is track-tight and weight-driven, never merely large. Leading compresses toward 1.0 as
  size grows.
- Body measure caps at 62ch (`--bk-measure-prose`); prose is the only thing that is measure-clamped.
- Body never below 1rem. The reading sizes start at 17px (`--bk-text-base`), rebased from the default
  because this sheet is read rather than glanced at.
- Headline sizes use `clamp()` so they scale continuously rather than stepping at breakpoints, and
  every fluid term is on `cqi`, never `vw` — an agent sidebar shrinks the body without changing the
  viewport, so a viewport-relative clamp reports a width this surface does not have.
- **One micro label, and it is not set in caps.** Every small word on the sheet — the word under a
  figure, a band head, a column name, the two sides of an identity pair — is one style:
  `--bk-label-size` (13px), sentence case, `--bk-track-label`, `--bk-label-ink`. Tracked-out
  uppercase at 12px in `--bk-graphite-3` was the sheet's default label and it cost legibility on
  every word in return for a drafting flavour the figure face and the rules already carry; the ink
  was also 3.8:1, under AA. `--bk-graphite-3` and `--bk-graphite-4` now carry rules, marks and
  disabled controls only — text stops at `--bk-graphite-2`.
- **One figure scale, at all three depths.** A figure and its label are `Stat`
  (`components/blocks/Stat.tsx`), at `--bk-text-md`, wherever it stands: the mast, the zone key,
  the block card, the dossier verdict. The arrangement changes between them; the scale does not.
  Four sizes of one read-out said the three depths were three instruments.
- **Every figure is monospace and tabular.** `taste-design` §3 makes this conditional on a Density
  dial; `blocks` does not carry one and takes the rule unconditionally, because a survey whose
  columns of counts do not align is not a survey.

## 4. Component Stylings — shared law

- **Buttons.** Tactile press (a 1px translate or a 0.98 scale), never an outer glow. One primary
  action per view.
- **The gate.** A gated action is a two-step arm-then-fire, and the two steps must be visually
  distinct states of one control, not two buttons. Arming is reversible and must be visibly
  escapable. This is the app's central visual idea and it gets the strongest treatment on the screen.
- **Evidence.** Confidence is never rendered as a bare percentage. It is always shown decomposed
  into the named rules that produced it, with each rule's weight, because confidence *is* the sum of
  those weights capped at 0.99 — it is not a model score. A direction that hides the evidence has
  lost the domain.
- **Cards.** Only where elevation communicates hierarchy. At Density > 7, cards are replaced by
  dividers, negative space or glass planes. `blocks` has no elevation to spend at all — `--bk-elev-0`
  is `none` — so its hierarchy is rule weight, inversion and hatch density.
- **Loading.** Skeletal, matching the real layout's dimensions. Never a circular spinner.
- **Empty states.** A composed illustration plus the action that populates it. Never "No data".
- **Inputs.** Label above, error below, real 2px focus ring — not a caret-only focus signal.
- **Targets.** Every interactive element ≥ 44px.

## 5. Hero / Entry Section

`taste-design` §5 assumes a marketing hero and prescribes inline image typography between words.
**Rejected for this app** — there are no photographs here and inventing them would violate the "no
fabricated content" ban in §9. What is adopted is the section's actual constraints:

- **The entry is the instrument, not an introduction to it.** `/` opens on the sheet itself,
  in survey order: sheet head, then the level rail and command bar, then the plate. There is no
  banner above the work.
- **One primary CTA.** No secondary "learn more".
- **No filler chrome.** No "scroll to explore", no bouncing chevrons, no scroll arrows.
- **No overlapping content.** Text never sits on top of other text or on an image. The one deliberate
  overlap is the arrival itself — the flattened plate and the cells that land on it are the same
  object mid-move, for about a second — and it is `aria-hidden` on the way out.

The entry section states plainly that **Athena is not connected to this app**, and describes her
capabilities in the present tense of the manifest — what she *would* be allowed to do and which of
those are gated — never in the past tense of work she has done.

## 6. Layout Principles — shared law

- CSS Grid for structure. `calc()` percentage math is banned.
- **The "three equal cards in a row" feature row is banned.** Use asymmetric splits, 2-column
  zig-zag, or a bento with unequal tracks. This is the exact failure mode the four original apps
  were rejected for, so it is enforced hard.
- No two vertically adjacent sections share a column structure.
- Content contained to a max width and centred; horizontal padding steps at three breakpoints.
- Full-height uses `100dvh` / `100svh`, never `100vh`.
- `z-index` only for header, overlay and modal contexts. No z-index spam.

## 7. Responsive Rules — shared law

Verified at **390, 768, 1280, 1920** (the brief's widths; `taste-design` names 375/768/1024/1440 and
the union is what gets checked).

- Every multi-column layout collapses to one column below 768px. No exceptions.
- **Horizontal overflow on mobile is a critical failure.** Wide content scrolls inside its own
  container, never the page body.
- Headlines scale by `clamp()`. Body never below 1rem.
- Touch targets ≥ 44px with real spacing between them.
- **Breakpoints are `@container`, never `@media (width…)`,** and every fluid term is on `cqi`, never
  `vw`: an agent sidebar shrinks the body without changing the viewport. A rule must also name the
  container its subject is actually inside — the dossier renders as a sibling of the sheet, not a
  child of it (`components/blocks/Blocks.tsx`), so a dossier rule queries `bk-dossier` or `bk-root`,
  and one that queries `bk-sheet` from in there can never match.
- A diagram is not a table. Where a wide diagram cannot shrink to legibility it degrades to a ranked
  list carrying the same encoding, rather than being drawn too small to read.

## 8. Motion & Interaction — shared law

`taste-design` §8 is the **only** part of either DESIGN.md format that supplies real motion values,
and its rules are adopted. Durations and easings are tokens (`--bk-dur-*`, `--bk-ease-*`).

- **Spring physics** for interactive state, `stiffness: 100, damping: 20` as the default feel. No
  linear easing anywhere. *Recorded divergence:* the live direction runs its transitions off named
  durations and cubic-bezier easings (`--bk-dur-*`, `--bk-ease*`) rather than springs, because the
  arrival's beats have to keep their order on a slow machine and a spring cannot be asked how long it
  will take. The "no linear easing" half binds regardless and is met.
- **Staggered orchestration.** Lists and grids never mount instantly; they cascade at an offset per
  index (`--bk-stagger`), capped so a long list does not take seconds to arrive.
- **Animate only `transform` and `opacity`.** Never `top`/`left`/`width`/`height`.
- **A move has to be followable.** The L0 → L1 arrival is four named beats and about three seconds,
  because a move nobody can watch is indistinguishable from a cut. The clock that owns the beats is
  `components/blocks/useArrival.ts` and the transitions they trigger are in `style/`; the two carry
  the same numbers and have to agree.
- **Perpetual micro-loops** on components that represent live agent state — and *only* those. This is
  where the app amends the skill: `taste-design` wants an infinite loop on every active component,
  but in a governed app a thing that pulses forever reads as "working". Nothing may imply Athena is
  working, because she is not connected. So a perpetual loop is permitted only on the async lane
  while a real job is running, and on nothing else.
- **Grain and noise** on fixed, `pointer-events: none` pseudo-elements only.
- Everything degrades under `prefers-reduced-motion`, which `app/globals.css` enforces at the root
  and the direction re-checks in script — `useReducedMotionQuery`
  (`components/blocks/cube/Turntable.tsx`) for the scene, and a `matchMedia` check before the arrival
  clock starts. Honouring the preference means arriving at the end state, never skipping the move and
  keeping the start state.
- Expensive perpetual layers are shed rather than left running. The live direction has none: the
  scene rebuilds its instance buffers only while something is actually moving
  (`components/blocks/cube/Records.tsx`), so a settled cube costs nothing.

## 9. Anti-Patterns (Banned)

`taste-design`'s list, kept nearly intact — it is a strict superset of the round-1 anti-slop list —
with the app's own additions marked.

- No emojis, anywhere.
- No `Inter`. No default serif stacks (`Times`, `Georgia`, `Garamond`, `Palatino`).
- No pure `#000000`.
- No neon outer glows. No oversaturated accents. No gradient text on large headers.
- No custom mouse cursors.
- No overlapping content layers.
- No three-equal-card feature rows.
- No centred hero at Variance > 4.
- No filler UI text: "scroll to explore", "swipe down", bouncing chevrons, scroll arrows.
- No AI copywriting clichés: "Elevate", "Seamless", "Unleash", "Next-Gen", "Revolutionize".
- No `LABEL // YEAR` typography.
- No circular loading spinners.
- No `h-screen`; use `min-h-[100dvh]` / `100svh`.
- **No fabricated data, metrics or statistics.** Never invent an uptime percentage, a response time,
  a satisfaction score or a round number. This one is not merely a taste rule here — it is repo law.
  Every number rendered in every direction comes from the seeded SQLite database through
  `lib/db.ts`, or it does not appear.
- **No fake "system metrics" sections** — no "BY THE NUMBERS" card grid filled with invented figures.
- **No generic placeholder names.** Not applicable by construction: all names are seeded.
- *(app)* **No claim that Athena did something.** She is not connected. Capabilities are described in
  the present tense of the manifest. No "Athena cleaned 120 records", no fake activity feed.
- *(app)* **No bare confidence percentage** without its evidence decomposition.
- *(app)* **No governance colour spent on decoration.** The governance roles mean one thing each.
- *(app)* **No mock data arrays inlined in a component.** Server components read `lib/db.ts`.
  This directly contradicts `stitch-build/react-components` Phase 3, which mandates moving all
  content into `src/data/mockData.ts`. The repo wins.

**Directions this document has claimed and the repo has never carried.** §0's table used to give
`signal` (6/8/6/3, `/v/signal`), `broadsheet` (7/6/5/2, `/v/broadsheet`), `daylight` (10/3/9/7,

The colour, type and motion sections above were written for `daylight` and `lattice` and are now
written for `blocks`. What did **not** change with them is §4 and §6–§9: those were shared law when
there were five directions and they are shared law with one, which is the whole reason they are
marked as such.

The DTCG token pipeline — `design/tokens/*.tokens.json` built by `design/build-tokens.mjs` to a
`[data-variant="…"]` layer — was the value authority for `signal`, `broadsheet` and `token`. `token`
was cut and the other two were never here, so the pipeline is **retired**: the emitter and its three
inputs each say so in their own first lines, no npm script runs it, and nothing imports its output.
`blocks` declares its values directly in `components/blocks/style/base/tokens.css`, which is what the
top of this file names as the authority — there is no `blocks.tokens.json` and there is not meant to
be one.

It is marked rather than deleted because `design/build-tokens.mjs` is the upstream of record for the
vendored copies in `examples/hirelane` and `examples/ledgerbox`, which document their divergence from
it line by line, and because it is the only implementation of `--refs`. `test/tokens.test.ts` fails if
the pipeline is quietly reconnected, if a marker is dropped, or if the emitter stops running. Wiring
`blocks` onto it is a direction, not a tidy-up: it would need a `blocks.tokens.json`, a `tokens`
script, a CI check that fails when the tree and the source diverge, and that test rewritten.
