The claim

Circle: when a parent needs care, the whole family works from one record, and Dad still decides.

For the families who share the care of an ageing parent. Today one person carries it all in their head and phone, the others can't help because they don't know what's going on or aren't allowed to act, and the parent slowly loses their say. Circle is one shared agent for the whole circle. Every fact in it cites who said it and where it came from, and every action names whose yes it needs.

63 MAmericans gave ongoing care to an adult or a child with complex needs in the past year: nearly 1 in 4 adults S
3 %of families where adult siblings split a parent's care equally. In 41%, one sibling does all or most of it S (sponsored study)
45 %of US hospitals asked about it told a caregiver to just use the patient's password S. That's what "shared access" looks like today
The primary carer puts some of it downOne photo of the kitchen counter turns into a checked medication list. One recorded family call turns into tasks that other people have claimed. Target: 30% fewer coordination hours a week A
The others can actually helpA sibling three states away sees the same cited record and can pick up work that fits their rights. Target: the non-primary share of tasks rises to 35% or more A
The parent keeps the last wordAnything that shares their records or spends their money waits for their signature on their own tablet, in large type, read aloud. No shared passwords.
Why Meta's models, and why now. The three hard inputs of a shared care record are who said it, what's actually in the photo and keeping it at home. Meta now offers all three: diarized streaming speech (muse-voice-transcribe-1.0, $0.18/h S), promptable segmentation of every instance in a photo or video (sam-3.1 S), and an Apache-2.0 multimodal agent model that runs on one consumer GPU (Muse Glimmer S), alongside Muse Spark's 1M-token multimodal reader. Other vendors sell some of these pieces S. Having all four from one vendor, under one API and one open-weights licence family, is new in 2026 A.

Tags on every load-bearing claim: S sourced (click it) · R recalled, unverified · A our assumption or judgement. On Meta models: press = published by Meta or in press coverage, unverified = not published or only reported second-hand. Nobody on the team has called the Meta Model API yet, so no Meta number below is measured.

Position

Why several people change everything: the six invariants, reread

Athena Portable is single-user by invariant (README.md §2). A circle breaks that on purpose. Of its six invariants, two are kept as they are, two are extended and two have to be rethought. The rethought ones are the design.

#Athena PortableIn CircleNew form
1Markdown on disk is truth; a brain is portable by copying the directoryrethoughtStill markdown on disk, now on the circle's hub. But "copy the folder" is a disclosure of someone else's health record, so export becomes a gated action that needs the principal (the parent) to sign.
2A fact must cite live episodes, or the write is refusedextendedA fact cites episodes and the people behind them: author, speaker, device. A medication fact must cite a document or a clinician's speech, never just a sibling saying so.
3Policy lives in the gate; a GATED tool never runs without a decisionrethoughtThe gate now answers whose decision. A rule table maps action × subject to a signer set: a role, a quorum, or the principal's veto. The model never picks the signers.
4Bounded and honest: (showing N of M)extendedHonest about what's withheld, too: (showing 6 of 9 · 3 visible only to Dad and Maya). A member knows something exists without seeing it.
5No provider is mandatorykeptKept by the open-weights route: Glimmer on the hub can run every role except speech and segmentation. Health content only ever uses Meta's Standard mode, never Contributor S.
6Cost is visible: one ledger row per model callextendedModel rows now carry the member who caused them, and a second ledger makes effort visible: who did which task. Cost was the single user's question. Fairness is the circle's.
Machine

The whole system on one page

Machine · part 1

The circle record: many writers, nothing ever overwritten

The record is Athena Portable's brain with one property doing all the work: episodes, facts and procedures are write-once. peer/replicate.py already relies on this, since "a device either has a memory, byte for byte, or it does not yet". Many members writing into one record therefore never need a merge. A correction is a new fact that supersedes an old one and cites the evidence for the change.

Machine · part 2

Read the counter: one photo becomes a checked medication list

The highest-stakes moment in shared care is coming home from hospital, when the discharge sheet and the bottles in the kitchen disagree (14.1% of older adults S). A generic model handed one photo of eight bottles reads some labels and blurs the others together. Circle splits the job: SAM 3.1 finds every instance of "pill bottle" and returns a mask for each S, each crop goes to Muse Spark on its own, and the reconciliation is plain code, not a model.

Machine · part 3

Voices make records. Devices make decisions.

Families decide things out loud: on speakerphone after an appointment, in the doctor's office, round the kitchen table. muse-voice-transcribe-1.0 does streaming transcription with speaker diarization, endpointing and keyword biasing S, for 20+ speakers S press. In Circle, diarization becomes an accountability primitive: "I'll call the pharmacy Thursday" becomes a task claimed by the person who said it.

Machine · part 4 · the core

The signer gate: every action knows whose yes it needs

Athena Portable's gate decides one thing: AUTO or GATED, from two flags, reversible and side_effects (contracts/manifest.py:153). Circle adds a third question, about whom, and turns the answer into a signer set. The model proposes the action. A rule table, which the principal and the primary carer edit and the model can't, decides who signs.

The rule table (excerpt)

-- signer_rule: edited only through a gated "change rules" action signed by principal + primary
action_class             subject   signers                          notes
read.record              any       none (view filter applies)       part 5 decides visibility
task.claim / task.done   circle    self                             effort ledger row
book.ride / book.visit   principal any helper + notify principal    reversible within 24 h
share.records.external   principal principal  OR  proxy_if_incapable  never a sibling alone
spend.principal_funds    principal primary + 1 other, > $100 adds principal
change.med_schedule      principal primary, AND a cited clinician source  agent never proposes doses
change.rights            circle    principal + primary              capacity change needs a POA document
export.record            circle    principal                        invariant 1, rethought
The same request, "send Dad's discharge summary to the new cardiologist", gets two different answers depending on who asks. From Dan it becomes a card on Dad's tablet. From Dad it simply goes. That difference is the product.
Machine · part 5

Views and the privacy line

Each member's agent turn is composed from their view of the record, never from the whole record with an instruction to "not mention" the rest. The paid aide sees today's care plan and the meds she gives, not the money or the sibling argument. Withholding happens in the composer, before any model sees anything. That matters because Glimmer's own published safety row shows a 26.4 "CI Memories violation" rate against 12.1 for Gemma 4 S press: a model shouldn't be trusted to keep a secret it has been shown.

Machine · part 6

The load loop: make the invisible work visible, then share it

The 41% / 3% imbalance S survives because nobody sees the work. Each week Circle shows the circle what's open, who claimed what, and what fell through, then proposes a split that matches each person's declared capacity and rights. People claim tasks. The agent never assigns them.

Data model

New tables sit beside Athena Portable's existing companion_approval and ledger tables in the hub's SQLite index. The markdown files stay the truth.

-- who is in the circle; each member is a paired device set (peer/registry.py rows)
CREATE TABLE circle_member (
  id            TEXT PRIMARY KEY,          -- mem_…
  display_name  TEXT NOT NULL,
  role          TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (role IN ('principal','primary','helper','aide','proxy')),
  devices       TEXT NOT NULL,             -- JSON list of peer_id; pairing = peer/pairing.py
  capacity_note TEXT,                      -- "weekends, no driving", self-declared
  active        INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 1
);

-- every episode now says who, through what, and who may see it
ALTER TABLE episode ADD COLUMN author_id    TEXT;   -- member who captured it
ALTER TABLE episode ADD COLUMN speaker_id   TEXT;   -- bound diarized speaker, nullable
ALTER TABLE episode ADD COLUMN source_kind  TEXT;   -- 'document'|'photo_crop'|'speech'|'message'|'clinician_speech'
ALTER TABLE episode ADD COLUMN audience     TEXT;   -- JSON list of roles or member ids

-- a decision needing several people: one parent row, one approval row per signature
CREATE TABLE signer_request (
  id            TEXT PRIMARY KEY,          -- sig_…
  action        TEXT NOT NULL,             -- "share.records.external"
  params_json   TEXT NOT NULL,             -- canonical, as approvals._canonical
  proposer_id   TEXT NOT NULL,
  subject_id    TEXT NOT NULL,             -- whose life it touches
  rule_id       TEXT NOT NULL,             -- which signer_rule resolved it
  quorum        INTEGER NOT NULL,
  veto_holder   TEXT,                      -- principal or documented proxy
  state         TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (state IN
                ('awaiting','quorum_met','executed','vetoed','declined','expired'))
);
ALTER TABLE companion_approval ADD COLUMN signer_request_id TEXT;
ALTER TABLE companion_approval ADD COLUMN signer_id         TEXT;  -- must be on a paired device of this member

-- invariant 6, extended: effort is visible too
CREATE TABLE effort_row (
  id            TEXT PRIMARY KEY,
  task_id       TEXT NOT NULL,
  member_id     TEXT NOT NULL,
  kind          TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (kind IN ('claimed','done','dropped','handed_over')),
  minutes_est   INTEGER,                   -- self-reported, labelled as such
  at            TEXT NOT NULL
);
ALTER TABLE ledger ADD COLUMN member_id TEXT;              -- who caused this model call

What it leaves behind is the web-app agent: tabs, the page bridge, DOM hands. Circle's work happens in kitchens and phone calls, not inside SaaS tabs. Business shape and cost per circle

Who pays

Not the stressed carer alone. Phase 1: a circle subscription at about $15/month, paid by the circle and splittable across siblings A. That's a small slice of ≈ $7,200 a year out of pocket S. Phase 2: employers. 6 in 10 caregivers work and half report disruption S, and caregiving benefits are an existing employer category R. Health plans come after there is outcome data, not before.

Distribution: the circle grows by invitation. One carer invites three people, so every customer is a channel A. The kill line measures exactly this.

Argument

What round 2 should prototype

1 · Dad's card

The principal's decision moment on a tablet: large type, read aloud, "Yes / Not yet / Ask Maya", with who asked and why. If an 80-year-old can't answer it in one go, the whole rights model fails. Test it with the ana UAT character (low vision) first, then with real older adults.

2 · The counter reveal

The photo with masks appearing, the count, then the reconcile rows with their citations, and how a red flag reads without causing panic. This is the demo's physical moment and the part where trust is won or lost.

3 · Dan's week

A far-away sibling's phone view: what's open, what fits his rights and capacity, a one-tap claim, and the private load mirror. It proves the spread target is reachable from a phone in two minutes a day.

Not for round 2

Hub console, rule-table editor, onboarding. They matter, but none of them proves the bet.
