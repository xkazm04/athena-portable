## 2. Design opportunities

Ranked by recurrence (none, first run), then convergence across Characters, then voice escalation (a voice harsher than its
finding row).

### D1. The approval path tells the user something false and loses the decision (build, critical)
- **Evidence:** mira-4, jonas-6, juror-9, mira-5; L2 live. With another origin focused, Approve showed `foreign_origin ... no session with this
  daemon` and `Approved. Stamped and sent to the record.` at once; the card vanished; the daemon kept the approval pending
  (`apr_23e30fb6cf68`) through a restart.
- **Voices (escalation):** Jonas: "the approval is logged as approved before the daemon has said so". Mira: "I approved a card from the wrong tab and it
  just vanished; the stamp said approved." Juror: "honesty when something fails after the stamp".
- **Hypothesis for root cause:** the answer is optimistic in the run store (`run.answer` removes the card first, L1 juror-9) and nothing reads
  `GET /decisions` back (`DaemonApi.decisions()` has no caller, L1 mira wiring audit). Verify against `run.ts` before fixing.
- **Cost/value:** small to medium code, very high trust value. It is the one place the product's whole promise is judged.
- **Guardrail (strength `uat-s2`):** keep the one-key answer, the stamp on a real answer, "Nothing runs until you sign".

### D2. A switch that says off and is not (build, critical)
- **Evidence:** jonas-1, mira-11; L2 live (store `enabled:false`, a turn still ran). Browser says "switched off; nothing runs here".
- **Voices:** Jonas: "That is the exact failure I came here to catch: a control that says off and is not." Mira: "the one that changes what I believe the product does".
- **Hypothesis:** the daemon's `Policy` takes `disabled_origins` but `wiring.py` never passes it (mira L1, `wiring.py:235-236`); the origin row is written and
  read only by two views (jonas L1). Ceiling: only a read-only turn was tried on a disabled origin.
- **Cost/value:** small; it is a policy wiring plus a test. Value is the product's invariant 3 on the user's most-touched control.

### D3. The card a user signs omits what is signed (concept-doc, then build; high)
- **Evidence:** mira-1, jonas-7, ana-2, juror-8; L2 live: one parameter (`ID inv_0029`), recipient named only in the model's rationale, no body, no origin.
- **Voices:** Mira: "Who is it going to? I do not know without opening the books ... For thirty-seven it is the whole job again." Jonas: card shows "about 5 of 12" fields.
- **Why concept-doc and not straight build:** what a card shows has to come from somewhere other than model prose, or the signature binds nothing. Options: the page tool
  declares a preview (recipient, subject, body) that the gate echoes; or the daemon re-reads the saved draft; or the card shows the tool's full parameters and a
  link to the evidence. Each changes the manifest contract (`contracts/`), so it needs an ADR and a test first (AGENTS.md "Contracts first").
- **Cost/value:** medium to large; it decides whether the 45-minute chore is ever faster than by hand (Mira's time-saved depends on it).

### D4. Closing Main quits everything under a label that says she stays (build, high)
- **Evidence:** mira-6, jonas-11, juror-6, priya-12; four Characters and L2 live. ADR 0026 chose "closing Main quits", the label contradicts it.
- **Cost/value:** trivial to fix the copy; the larger question (should she outlive Main, with the tray as her home) is a product decision for the owner. Build the
  copy now; record the behaviour choice in the next ADR.

### D5. First launch does not get the user to a first app, and the engine is asserted, not checked (build, high)
- **Evidence:** priya-1, priya-2, priya-3, priya-5, priya-6, mira-9, mira-10, juror-1, juror-4; L2 live for the unwired probe and the empty Browser.
- **Voices:** Priya: "Then it told me, with a green tick, that my engine is Claude Code. I pay for ChatGPT." Juror: "The first three claims I read are not wired."
- **Hypothesis (verified at L1 and by L2 text):** the probe exists in Python (`engines.py`), but no daemon route or IPC carries it, and Setup passes `probes: null`.
- **Ceiling:** what a **missing** engine shows was not built at L2; the claim "it shows the wrong thing" is L1 plus the live "has not answered yet".

### D6. The Record contradicts itself and cannot answer "which site" (build, high)
- **Evidence:** jonas-5, juror-11, mira (Record), uat-1; L2 live: "Nothing is missing" above "Nothing has run in this window yet"; no site or tool; times in UTC.
- **Voice:** Jonas: "Where is the log? There is one, and it is a window-run scratchpad."
- **Cost/value:** medium. The daemon ledger has more fields than the UI keeps (8 of 17, jonas L1 hypothesis).

### D7. Time zones: UTC shown as local (build, high, small)

## 3. Methodology lessons

1. **The skill did not parse.** Its `description` was an unquoted YAML scalar containing `: `, so `/uat` was unknown in the Claude CLI. Fixed in the registry (quoted, version 1.11.1).
   Two other registry skills (`friend`, `npm-updates`) fail the same parse; they are untouched and need their own fix.
2. **The skill was never installed in this repo.** Reading `SKILL.md` from the registry path is not installing it. `scripts/link-registry.mjs` and a `.claude/skills/<name>` link are.
   A first run that read the skill by path "worked" but left `/uat` unavailable to the next session.
3. **No desktop (Tauri) driver exists in the skill.** The L2 driver here attaches to WebView2 over CDP. Measured: browsed tabs are **not** CDP targets of the chrome session; use the shell's own commands
   for them. Real pixels need an OS crop, and a crop of a window rectangle can contain the user's other windows (privacy): prune Main-rectangle crops.
4. **Vacuous and mine-not-theirs checks.** More than a dozen of my own checks were void (a selector matching "off" inside "Switch off"; a wrong state predicate; a 401 read as "none pending"; a wrong JSON key; a precondition
   broken by my own cleanup). The driver should assert and print its preconditions, and a 401/empty result must never pass.
5. **L1 convergence predicted L2 well** (19 of 19 driveable findings confirmed, 0 refuted). The two big converged findings that L2 could not prove (focus theft, 150%) were environment arms. This supports using L1
   convergence to choose what to build the L2 rig for, rather than to skip L2.
6. **The fixture lied.** The README's demo ids do not exist in the example app, and ATHENA_STORE does not isolate the brain. Both are now in `env.md`; the second is finding uat-4.
7. **A real-engine L2 costs minutes and money per card.** Budget it. A scripted-engine path through the shell (the daemon already has `--transcript`) would make J3 and J2 cheap and deterministic (M1).
