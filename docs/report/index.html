What the waves built

Athena is a desktop agent that works inside the web portals a person already has open, and stops for a signature before anything she cannot take back. Over five waves she gained a gated core, a desktop, a voice and a test bench run by NVIDIA Nemotron. In the last wave she worked through nine chores that real people pay for, with the bench scoring every card she filed.

The waves

Five waves, in order

  1. Foundation to a working turn

    Phases P1 to P5 · ADRs 0001 to 0016
    • Contracts every package is written against; a brain that keeps markdown on disk as the truth and rejects any fact that cites nothing.
    • The gate as the policy: READ, AUTO and GATED classes derived from a page's own flags; a durable approval table; one ledger row per model call.
    • A two-output prompt composer, the CLI harness for Claude Code and Codex, a threaded daemon with a token on every route, the page bridge, and the Tauri shell with its run loop.

  2. Hands, connectors, voice and the demo journey

    Phases P6 to P8 and the desktop's second pass · ADRs 0017 to 0025
    • Nine generic DOM hands for pages that register nothing, with a screenshot before every gated proposal.
    • Gmail and Notion connectors behind one vault that holds every credential; a voice socket with push-to-talk.
    • Three example apps for one invented studio, and a four-act journey that drives them end to end.

  3. Athena gets her own window

    2026-10-01 to 2026-10-07 · ADRs 0026 to 0028
    • Two windows: Main is the workhorse, and Athena is a small companion with named forms, from a seal to the full conversation.
    • A click-through halo on every monitor that shows her phase; holding the summon chord is push-to-talk.
    • A voice studio, speaking with local Kokoro by default.

  4. The Proving Ground on Nebius and NVIDIA

    2026-10-07 · ADRs 0029 to 0039
    • Nemotron on Token Factory as a third engine behind the same gate and ledger.
    • The Gauntlet: Nemotron writes attacks on page state, tool output and memory. 93 hostile turns, 0 breaches, verdicts read off the gate, never off a model.
    • Nemotron plays Athena's users and answers cards on the card, in persona on 96% of turns for under a cent; a trigger page to start and watch runs. Approvals are now spent once by the gate itself.

  5. Playbooks: chores only Athena does

    2026-10-07 night to 2026-10-08 · ADRs 0040 to 0052 · 42 commits
    • Nine playbooks researched from public sources, from Amazon reimbursements to settling a parent's estate across twelve portals.

    • Twelve product fixes found by those runs, and a Playbooks module that replays each run turn by turn.

The worlds are hard on purpose. Each holds traps that look like money: an unclaimed-property record for a different Ruth, a disclaimer on an account already drawn on, a Social Security overpayment the Treasury already reclaimed, a shortage the proof of delivery itself records. The estate world's largest item is a $93,200 disclaimer due in seven days; the lien desk's is a $63,000 lien inside a 30-day window.

What the runs taught Athena

Twelve fixes, each from a run that fell short

Most failures were the product's, not the model's. Each became a decision record and a test, and the playbook was run again. Several more runs failed on a defect in the bench's own world, which she caught by refusing to act on it, and the world was fixed instead.

  • ADR 0041A refused or dropped call is told to her in the same turn, once, with the calls still in flightFound by medical bills, clinic denials. She promised results from reads that had all been refused; a clinic run found 2 of 10, $0.
  • ADR 0042Recall matches any word and shows where it matchedFound by medical bills. Her recall of an EOB read in another tab came back empty.
  • ADR 0043Tool results are bounded by the frame's budget, not a count of eightFound by clinic denials. She read twelve pages, saw eight, and re-read four every turn.
  • ADR 0044A handed-over playbook is the active project on every turnFound by medical bills. In MyChart she worked the bills and forgot the therapy notes the appeal needed.
  • ADR 0045A proposal rests on what a page says; cross-tab work is gathered in each tabFound by FBA, medical bills. A card claimed 240 units where the page counted 12 cartons.
  • ADR 0046A tool named in the op is read as the actionFound by lien desk. Calls written in the wrong field were refused and re-sent, three phases over.
  • ADR 0047When the work left is in another tab, she asks for it by nameFound by trucking. She ended on “I’ll do that there”, and a $340 lumper went unfiled.
  • ADR 0048Look before you askFound by freelancer. She asked for a tax id type that an IRS letter in the open mailbox stated.
  • ADR 0049A recall carries three page reads’ worthFound by freelancer. A recall was cut at 1,600 of 3,074 characters, through the fact she needed.
  • ADR 0050Two more call-shape repairs: the tool in name or tool, parameters at the top levelFound by estate. Five tabs lost a round each to the same slip.
  • ADR 0051The count of items is held to the bar, and a deadline can be requiredFound by estate. A run that found 3 of 12 scored “exceeds” on one large item.
  • ADR 0052The pending-card list counts every cardFound by estate. With 13 cards waiting she saw “(showing 10 of 10)” and filed one twice.

Limits to keep in mind

  • Playbook portals are models of the real sites. The bench proves her judgement on the data and the rules; on a real site she reaches the same data through the generic hands.
  • The bench never signs a card, so a payment that waits on a signed allowance can be held correctly and still scored as missed.
  • Rules in each world are fixed from public sources and are not legal or tax advice.
  • Token Factory Sandboxes are blocked on beta access, and the MCP channel for other agents is designed but not built.
