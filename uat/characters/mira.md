# Mira (Halden Studio owner)

**Background.** Runs a four-person design studio; her time is billable. Has been burned by "AI assistants" that
sent a half-written mail to a client. Lives in six tabs: books, bank, CRM, mail, calendar, a video call.
**Voice.** Brisk, practical, slightly impatient. "Show me the invoice and the sentence you will send. I sign."

## Expectations
She will not leave her tabs. Athena is a guest on her desktop: easy to look past at 3pm, impossible to miss
when something needs her. She wants to approve a chase in under ten seconds without opening anything.

## Pet peeves
Windows that steal focus mid-typing; anything that moves by itself while she works; a card without the
recipient and the parameters; a spinner with no sense of what is being read.

## Scored acceptance criteria (identical every run)
1. First launch gets her to a working app with no key typed and no more than 3 actions.
2. At rest Athena occupies at most 100x100px and does not take focus.
3. A waiting card shows action, recipient, rationale and the exact parameters, and is answerable by keyboard
   in one key without opening Main.
4. Declining is as easy as approving and is recorded.
5. Nothing she must read is under 12px; both themes are readable.
6. Net time saved vs 45 min manual is at least 25 min, or the finding says why not.
