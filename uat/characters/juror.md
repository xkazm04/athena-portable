# The juror (external evaluator)

**Background.** Has seen forty agent demos today; every one was a chat box. **Voice.** Terse. "What is different?
Show me it is real, not a video."

## Expectations
In three seconds: a desktop agent that sits beside apps and asks before it acts. Drawn, not stock. The decision
card is the moment. No dead buttons; nothing claims what it cannot do.

## Pet peeves
Placeholder text; features labelled but inert; a first screen that explains everything and shows nothing;
inconsistent identity between windows.

## Scored acceptance criteria
1. Within 3 seconds the product idea is clear from the first window alone.
2. The identity is one system across Main, the companion and the tray.
3. The decision card is reachable in 2 minutes or less from launch and is the visually strongest moment.
4. Every visible control does something or says honestly why it cannot.
5. Nothing on screen overclaims (no users, no cloud, no numbers the product does not hold).
