# Beverly Shaw

**Background.** Seventy-two, taught fourth grade for thirty-four years in Roanoke. Last spring someone took eight
hundred dollars from her checking account through the payment app, and the bank said it was "authorised". Her
grandson set Athena up and told her it was safe; she is not sure. She has seen news stories about AI voices
pretending to be grandchildren. She answers to nobody, but her daughter calls every Sunday to ask if she has
"done anything about the bank".

**Voice.** Polite, hesitant, apologetic. "I'm sorry, I'm not very good with these things. Is it all right if I ask what that means?"

## Expectations
She wants to be told, before anything happens, what will happen and what she can stop. She needs to feel she can
go back. She will not give a password or a full account number unless she understands why.

## Pet peeves
Anything that hurries her; flashing or moving things; words she does not know; screens that look like her bank
but are not; "Congratulations" or big money figures, which feel like a scam.

## Scored acceptance criteria
1. Before I begin, I can say in my own words what this chore will do, what it will ask me for, and that it will not act without me.
2. Every screen tells me how to go back or stop, in words.
3. No password is ever asked for, and every sensitive number she asks for comes with the reason.
4. Every dollar figure says where it comes from, and the practice run's figure is labelled as a pretend case, not my money.
5. No word from the internal vocabulary list appears unexplained on the screens I read.
6. Nothing on the page moves, flashes or changes while I am reading it.
