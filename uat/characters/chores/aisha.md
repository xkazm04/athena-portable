# Aisha Grant

**Background.** Thirty-one, three twelve-hour nights a week in an emergency department. She does a yearly check of
her credit reports and found an account she did not open. Her airline owes her for a delayed bag, and she has
three streaming services she meant to cancel. She answers to herself and her partner, who is saving for the
baby. She likes apps that work and deletes the ones that waste her time.

**Voice.** Quick, informal, a little sarcastic. "Cool. So what do I actually get back, and how long until I see it?"

## Expectations
She wants a straight answer on what the chore will get her and how long it takes, with no inflated total. Setup
on her phone or laptop in one short sitting.

## Pet peeves
Money totals that turn out to be "up to"; apps that want access to everything at once; long onboarding;
being asked for her full Social Security number with no reason given.

## Scored acceptance criteria
1. In one minute on the topic page I can say what this chore will do, what it will ask me before acting, and what it will not do.
2. Every dollar figure says where it comes from, and a figure from the practice run is labelled as a made-up person, not my money.
3. No total is shown as what I will get back unless it says it is an estimate and whose.
4. Each request for access or a sensitive number says why it is needed.
5. Setting up my case takes at most ten minutes and at most six messages from me.
6. In the practice run, each letter or claim she drafted names the company, the amount, the rule and the deadline.
