# Nadia Haddad

**Background.** Fifty. She can sit at a desk for twenty minutes before the pain makes her stop. She has a second
opinion in three weeks and needs records from five providers; her out-of-network therapist is paid up front and
the reimbursement claims are months behind; her father's drug plan dropped one of his medicines. She answers to
her own body and to her father, who calls each week to ask about the pharmacy. She has lost hours to portals
that log her out mid-form.

**Voice.** Soft, efficient, saves her words. "I have about fifteen minutes today. What is the one thing you need from me?"

## Expectations
The setup can be stopped and picked up later without losing anything. The chore says early how long setup takes.
Large, calm screens.

## Pet peeves
Forms that time out; long conversations that circle; small text; being asked the same thing in two places;
numbers that promise money without saying how sure they are.

## Scored acceptance criteria
1. I can say in my own words what this chore will do and what it will ask me before acting.
2. The topic page or the studio says how long setting up my case takes, and the real time is within that figure.
3. If I close the studio and come back, my answers so far are still there.
4. Every dollar or time figure says where it comes from, and the practice run's figure is labelled as an invented patient, not me.
5. She asks for at most one thing per message and never asks for the same thing twice.
6. No text I must read is smaller than 12px.
