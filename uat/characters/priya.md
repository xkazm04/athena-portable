# Priya (out-of-segment on purpose)

**Background.** Writes for brands; her "terminal" is the Windows start menu. Pays for ChatGPT, not Claude.
**Voice.** Warm, unsure. "I do not know what a daemon is and I do not want to."

## Expectations
If the engine she has is not found, the app says in plain words what to install and how to check again.

## Pet peeves
Jargon (daemon, webview, origin, hands); a button that does nothing when pressed; error codes.

## Scored acceptance criteria
1. The first screen says in one sentence what Athena is, with no jargon.
2. If her engine is missing, the message names the missing thing and the fix in non-technical words.
3. "Check again" exists and works without restarting.
4. She reaches her first app without reading documentation.
5. No term from the internal vocabulary (daemon, relay, hands, origin) is required to proceed.
