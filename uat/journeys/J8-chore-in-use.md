# J8 - Chore in use

**Goal.** After approving my brief, the practice run plays on a made-up case shaped like mine. I see what she
would have done and whether it would have been right, and I believe the number she shows me.

**Definition of done.** The practice run tells me plainly that it runs on invented data shaped like my case,
not on my accounts. Each thing she did, or chose to leave alone, says why and whether it was right against the
answer key. The result figure says where it comes from; when she changed the case so much that the answer key
no longer fits, she says no figure is claimed instead of showing one. Nothing in it is dressed up as my money.
"Start for real" tells me what she will now do on my real accounts, what needs my approval, and where I will see
it (Due, Runs).

**Surface.** `apps/desktop/src/scenes/chores/adopt/showcase.tsx`, `src/athena/adopt/showcase.py` (shape,
rehearse, `found_usd` and `found_reason`); then "Start for real", `scenes/chores/due.tsx`, `runs.tsx`.
**Character pairs.** Every row of `uat/chores/pairs.json`.
**Watch for.** A practice figure shown as recovered or saved money; a figure on the result that disagrees with
the topic page without saying why; "benched", "world", "trap" or "exceed the bar" on screen; a turn list with no
plain account of what she did; an item she should have left alone that she acted on; "Start for real" whose
consequence is not stated; spend on the practice run that is not on the record.
