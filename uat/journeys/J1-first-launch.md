# J1 - First launch

**Goal (user POV).** I just installed Athena. I want to know what it is, that it works on my machine, and
to have my first app open and readable by her, without typing a key or reading a manual.

**Definition of done.** I see one welcome, the engine she will use is named and found (or I am told plainly
what is missing and how to fix it), I open my first app, Main appears with that app, and Athena settles
beside it as a small resting form. Nothing I did not ask for opened.

**Surface.** `athena.html` welcome state; `settings.onboarded`; `companion::after_onboarded`; Setup module.
**Watch for.** Main hidden on first launch (is the user left with one window and a clear next step?); the
"Open your first app" action when no app address was entered; Codex-only machines; what "Later (Esc)" does.
