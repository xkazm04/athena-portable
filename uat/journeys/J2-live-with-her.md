# J2 - Live with her

**Goal.** She is on my desktop all day. I want her out of the way when I work, to find her when I need her,
to move her where I like, to put her away, and to never lose her.

**Definition of done.** At rest she is small and quiet; I can drag her, dock her to an edge or to Main, hide
her, and bring her back from the tray or a chord; hiding does not make me miss a waiting decision; her
position and state survive what I did.

**Surface.** `companion.rs` (snap, follow, tab), `src/companion/machine.ts`, tray, `Ctrl+Shift+Space`, status pill in Main.
**Watch for.** Focus theft; where she sits at 125/150% and on a second monitor; a tab dragged back off an
edge; what Main's close button does to her; resting-state legibility.
