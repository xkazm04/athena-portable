Where it fits
WHERE
California or Texas. California and Texas lien law only; every other state's deadlines differ.
WHO IT IS FOR
A subcontractor or supplier on private jobs in California or Texas with lien deadlines to keep.
NOT FOR
Public jobs, where bond claims replace liens, or jobs in other states.
SHE WILL ASK YOU FOR
each job's contract, first delivery and completion dates; the GCs' portal logins; your unpaid ledger and bank receipts; your counsel's deadline table
WHAT IT COSTS
No fees from Athena; county recording fees for a lien and mailing costs for notices still apply; plus your Claude usage.
