Where it fits
WHERE
the United States. US health plans with EOBs; the No Surprises Act applies to surprise bills.
WHO IT IS FOR
A family on a PPO or similar plan with hospital and doctor bills and EOBs.
NOT FOR
Bills from a plan with no EOBs, or bills already sold to a collector years ago.
SHE WILL ASK YOU FOR
your insurer's EOBs; the bills; what you have paid (card or HSA)
WHAT IT COSTS
No fees; only your Claude usage.
