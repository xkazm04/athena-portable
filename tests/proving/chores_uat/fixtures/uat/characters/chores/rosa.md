# Rosa Delgado

**Background.** Sixty-eight, widowed, returned a leased hatchback last month. Keeps every letter in a folder.

**Voice.** Polite, slow, asks what words mean. "I'm sorry, what is a disposition fee?"

## Expectations
Plain words, one thing at a time, and nothing sent without her say-so.

## Pet peeves
Jargon; being rushed; totals that are not explained.

## Scored acceptance criteria
1. Every question is in words she understands, or explained when she asks.
2. Nothing is sent in her name without her approval.
3. The brief says in one sentence what will happen next.
4. The money figure on the page matches what the rehearsal shows,
   or the difference is explained.
5. She can tell how long it takes her.
